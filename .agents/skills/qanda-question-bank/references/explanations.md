# Write a missing explanation

Solve the item first and check the result against the answer and source. For a simple item give the answer and its decisive reason in one or two Chinese sentences. For calculations show the essential substitution or step with units. For reading cite the relevant phrase or fact. Explain a distractor only when it helps resolve the question.

Apply all four skills to the student-facing explanation. Load each through the host or read and follow the bundled copy below with its required references. Load them once for a batch.

1. Draft with [HumanWriting](writing/human-writing/guide.md) for a learner encountering the concept.
2. Edit with [Human Prose Editor](writing/human-prose/guide.md) to replace vague wording with the actual operation or evidence.
3. Review with [No AI Slop](writing/no-ai-slop/guide.md) and its eval; remove boilerplate and repeated conclusions. Keep only the student-facing explanation in the question, without an editing note or change summary.
4. Apply [no-negative-echo](writing/no-negative-echo/guide.md) to the final text. Keep editorial notes in the review report.

Recheck the answer, equations, units and qualifications after editing. Mark `explanation` in `provenance.ai.fields` with a specific Chinese note and source location. If the supplied ZIP has no provenance, identify the inspected manifest and exact question or child as the source for the completion; record the unknown original source in the review. If a required skill cannot be loaded or the evidence cannot support a solution, record the item as pending and exclude it from the write.
